# What the reviewers challenged

This folder holds the public versions of **Fable’s** independent technical reviews of Venkatesa Chandrasekaran’s *An algebraic quantization of gravity from the null cone* ([arXiv:2611.34924v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34924v1), 25 November 2026) and of this study’s own derivations.

Each review was drafted by **Astra** from Fable’s raw reviewer notes and edited by **Neon** for public clarity, preserving the distinction between what Fable concluded and what the editors treat as conditional. Earlier raw Fable notes are private, unedited source material superseded by the public records here.

> **Independence and review status.** The preprint acknowledges collaboration with GPT-6 Astra on several calculations and proofs. Fable belongs to a different model family from Astra and wrote the initial objections before reading the later audit notes. Neither Fable’s reviews nor this study’s cross-checks have yet had a human specialist referee. An exact, reproducible calculation or explicit counterexample therefore carries more weight than agreement between models.

## How to read the verdicts: gap, defect, or physical refutation

Every objection in this review falls into one of three distinct categories:

- **Proof gap.** The paper asserts a step without proving it. The underlying claim may still turn out to be true.
- **Defect as written.** Two statements in version 1 cannot both hold under a stated reading, so the mathematical construction has to change. The physical conjecture itself remains open.
- **Physical refutation.** Evidence that the physical claim is false of four-dimensional quantum gravity. **Nothing in these reviews is a physical refutation.** In particular, no calculation here proves that finite-dimensional causal diamonds are physically impossible.

Model calculations—such as linearized radial clocks, one-pair uncertainty examples, or two-dimensional Jackiw–Teitelboim (JT) gravity—test specific inference rules and supply controls. They are kept strictly separate from theorems about interacting four-dimensional quantum gravity.
