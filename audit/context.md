# What the null-cone proposal adds, and what others have checked

**The physical question.** *What is genuinely new in the null-cone proposal for four-dimensional quantum gravity, and how have outside technical checks and specialist critics responded so far?*

**The short answer.** Venkatesa Chandrasekaran's strongest novelty claim is combining finite-coupling dynamics, region gluing, and a finite state count inside a single Lorentzian construction of four-dimensional vacuum Einstein gravity. Each ingredient has direct precedents in earlier work on null-surface quantization, horizon algebras, and holography. As of 26 November 2026—less than a day after `arXiv:2611.34924v1` was submitted at 18:58 UTC on 25 November—there is already published qualitative specialist criticism from Jonathan Oppenheim, alongside two public technical reports and a public explainer. None of these is an independent full reconstruction of the continuum gravitational construction, which remains a separate, open question. [Version 1 and submission history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34924v1)

## Closest earlier results

| Earlier result | What this changes about the claim of novelty |
| --- | --- |
| Reisenberger derived the classical brackets on free data on two intersecting null hypersurfaces and related them to the Peierls bracket on regular observables. Fuchs and Reisenberger later simplified those brackets and made progress on a deformation quantization under cylindrical symmetry, explicitly leaving a general quantization and Hilbert-space representation open. [Classical null brackets](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.2541) · [2017 quantization study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.06992) | The two null surfaces, unconstrained classical data, and their canonical brackets were already available. The 2026 paper proposes a concrete quantum representation, Hamiltonian evolution, and an interpretation of its boundary states for general four-dimensional radiation. |
| Chandrasekaran and Flanagan derived a phase space for intersecting null surfaces, including a relative boost conjugate to area. Their subsequent work quantized perturbations of a black-hole horizon and obtained a type II∞ algebra for each horizon cut. Freidel and Kirklin also quantized gravitational degrees of freedom on an individual null ray using a clock built from the gravitational field. [Horizon phase spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03871) · [Subregion algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07915) · [Quantized null ray](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02228) | Boundary reference frames, area as a boost generator, and gravitational clocks all have direct precedents. The proposed type I algebra describes a different region, algebra, and representation; proving a limit relating the two algebra types would provide a further test. |
| Wieland obtained a discrete area spectrum for null cuts from a continuum boundary Fock representation in 2017. In 2024 he nonperturbatively quantized an impulsive sector with step-function shear along each ray, arbitrary angular dependence, and a Holst term. In 2025 he represented the null constraint algebra and radiative shear through a current algebra. [Null-cut area](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.00479) · [Impulsive radiation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.17491) · [Current algebra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10802) | Both nonperturbative null quantization and discrete null-cut area predate v1. Here the claimed route to discreteness is finite rank after bounding a gravitational clock on a macroscopic diamond, which yields no numerical list or spacing of allowed areas. Comparing directly with Wieland would require identifying the same boundary conditions, action, and observable. |
| Andrade e Silva and Jacobson quantized the reduced phase space of diamonds in pure 2+1 gravity. Theofilis and Wieland then gave a general prescription for diamond amplitudes from quantum null slabs, conditional on symmetry vacua and a projector onto physical states. [2+1 gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.10084) · [Null-slab amplitudes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.09679) | The current proposal aims to supply interacting local gravitational radiation in 3+1 dimensions and to construct the evolution and physical states that a general gluing prescription needs. |
| Finite Hilbert spaces for bounded diamonds and area-controlled state counts are longstanding holographic proposals. Banks, Draper, and Farkas recovered an area contribution from Euclidean diamond boundary terms. Colafranceschi, Dong, Marolf, and Wang obtained type I algebras and ordinary traces from axioms on an ultraviolet-complete Euclidean gravitational path integral with asymptotic AdS boundaries. [Holographic space-time](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08205) · [Euclidean diamond entropy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.03449) · [Axiomatic type I result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02189) | The new claim is to derive finite dimension for a clock-bounded subspace using Lorentzian gravitational preparations, and horizon entropy using a trace over its states. A finite entropy for one state alone places no upper bound on Hilbert-space dimension. |

## Public technical checks and specialist criticism

1. **A numerical and mathematical audit of §§4.4 and 5.2.** The SHIN09-git release reproduces the one-polarization, one-angular-harmonic Gaussian overlap and identifies a substantive gap. Resolving the identity between two fixed, normalizable states bounds an open amplitude, whereas closing the final cut sums diagonal elements over an entire basis. The report constructs a two-area operator model in which exhaustive orderings give different limits for a signed closed sum, flagging the missing argument that the specified gravitational cutoffs converge to the uncut amplitude. The example concerns the logical sufficiency of open gluing. Its authorship statement credits Codex with the derivations and reports no external specialist review. [Release at commit 44df2c1](https://github.com/SHIN09-git/null-cone-quantum-gravity-verification/tree/44df2c1e4915c0e654dd5907d91b775e83f31326) · [Closed-cut derivation and technical report](https://github.com/SHIN09-git/null-cone-quantum-gravity-verification/blob/44df2c1e4915c0e654dd5907d91b775e83f31326/phase2/report/report.pdf)

2. **A finite-arithmetic certificate.** Lluis Eriksson's version appeared at 09:32 UTC on 26 November 2026. Its Lean source formalizes a bound on finite partial tails of the inverse-square series, a lower bound on dyadic blocks of the harmonic series, and exact arithmetic for the entropy coefficients. For example, the focusing lemma takes the focusing inequality as a hypothesis, and the Sen lemma verifies a product of integers once the heat-kernel inputs are supplied. These are genuine checks with clearly defined boundaries. The author operates the publishing platform, discloses AI assistance and model screening, and notes that the report is not peer-reviewed. I inspected the pinned statements without rerunning the external suite. [Eriksson's report and disclosures](https://airr.science/papers/ARR-2026-4VGGGBES9N9QKRJ9/versions/v1/) · [Lean statements at commit 57ad447](https://github.com/lluiseriksson/null-cone-quantum-gravity-certificates/blob/57ad447de34724c676eafdc6fa325cd8eb211520/lean/NullConeQGCert.lean)

3. **Published qualitative criticism by a quantum-gravity specialist.** On 26 November 2026, Jonathan Oppenheim published a qualitative critique in *Post Quantum* arguing that the paper operates within reduced phase-space canonical quantization without resolving its longstanding obstacles—specifically operator-ordering ambiguity, dependence on the choice of classical phase-space reduction, and unchecked equivalence to Dirac quantization. Oppenheim also reports asking a linked swarm of Claude agents to evaluate the paper against 21 canonical-quantization obstacles, stating that he reviewed enough of it to be confident in the overall refutation while allowing that mistakes may remain in the details. He organizes his evaluation around three core checks:
   - **Microscopic state count and closed sewing:** the paper does not count black-hole microstates directly, and Oppenheim argues that its finite-dimensional Hilbert-space proof assumes what it sets out to show (matching the unproved closed-sewing / finite-trace input isolated in our audit of §5.2).
   - **Diffeomorphism invariance:** rather than proving that the quantized theory retains the symmetry of general relativity, the paper imposes diffeomorphism invariance as an axiom.
   - **Physical Hamiltonian and recovery of general relativity:** the claimed semiclassical limit to general relativity relies on a physical Hamiltonian operator that the paper never constructs.
   [Oppenheim's critique in *Post Quantum*](https://superposer.substack.com/p/has-ai-come-for-quantum-gravity)

A public explainer separately checked the conversion to Sen's coefficient and the dilogarithm asymptotic, while expressly excluding the phase space, representation, finiteness argument, and determinants from its scope. At lookup on 26 November 2026, the public INSPIRE record had zero indexed citations and no journal reference. Thus specialist criticism and targeted public checks already exist, even though searches of the exact title, arXiv identifier, and public GitHub repositories found no independent full reconstruction of the continuum construction and no empirical discrimination. These early responses show concretely why an initial citation count misses active scrutiny, and why qualitative criticism and isolated checks remain distinct from a complete independent reconstruction. ## Physical consequences and discriminating tests

- **Graviton interactions.** The finite overlap in (4.33) probes the chosen Gaussian widths and source displacement. Even setting the phase to one leaves a convergent inverse-square series. A stronger comparison would prepare scattering wave packets, recover the standard asymptotically flat helicity amplitudes at low energy, and determine their finite-coupling continuation. The known two-loop four-graviton scale dependence associated with the cubic-curvature counterterm is one specific target. Equation (4.33) does not compute that process. [Paper §4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS4) · [Goroff and Sagnotti](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(86)90193-8) · [Four- and five-graviton comparison](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.02934)
- **Information in a finite region.** If the clock projector has finite nonzero rank, the diamond has a maximum number of mutually orthogonal preparations and finitely many possible corner areas in that window. Equation (5.40) expresses this result as a direct sum over the allowed areas. Computing the individual eigenvalues and multiplicities requires more information. One could test the claim against the regulator by tracking the rank and areas as more radiative modes enter, with fixed physical clock and boundary data. Footnote 20 leaves equivalence across choices of the reference state open. Exact state counts, area-dependent transition strengths and a detector model would be needed to infer emission or absorption lines. [Paper §§5.1–5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS2)
- **Black-hole entropy.** In Sen's pure-gravity mixed ensemble, mass is fixed and angular momenta are summed. His (1.4) gives 212/45 times the logarithm of a length, hence 106/45 times the logarithm of area. This is a nontrivial ensemble-sensitive low-energy check. Both calculations use the massless graviton and ghost content. A more independent microscopic check would count the area eigenstates and their multiplicities from the proposed Hamiltonian and compare with the thermal trace. Sen also supplies distinct targets when angular momentum is restricted. [Paper §5.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS3) · [Sen, especially equations (1.1)–(1.5)](https://arxiv.org/html/1205.0971v2)
- **Other observables.** The paper infers finite multiplicities below a fixed energy for specified reflecting AdS boundary conditions and relates connected and entangled disconnected fillings in selected horizon correlators. These give concrete questions for an independent calculation. The four-dimensional construction includes vacuum gravity, the Section 2 scalar belongs to a two-dimensional example, and the one-loop equation describes graviton backreaction on a smooth background. Quantitative predictions for matter, a full four-dimensional evaporation history or a cosmological observable would each require additional dynamics. I found no experimental result that selects this proposal over alternatives. [Paper §§2, 5 and 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1)
