# Can the clock-selected diamond support the proposed observables?

**The short answer.** *A finite-dimensional clock subspace can coexist with infinite-dimensional canonical commutation relations if the clock is only checked before and after a sequence of sources, and it can carry the one-parameter relative boost cleanly. The sharper kinematic test is whether a continuously varying local corner shape—which classically commutes with the clock—survives in the physical quotient.*

The canonical commutation relations in §4 act on an infinite-dimensional space, whereas §5 imposes the clock projection before and after an entire sequence of sources. The space of allowed endpoint states can therefore have finite rank without representing the continuum canonical relations directly. A more pointed referee question is whether the clock can eliminate a continuously fluctuating distribution of corner area that commutes with it. This can be tested within a single fixed-total-area sector, independently of how the closed gravitational path integral is normalized. [Manuscript, §§4.2–4.3 and 5.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS2.p2.1) · [Endpoint projection, Eq. (5.20)](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.E20)

## Does a single corner observable already require infinitely many states?

At fixed total area $\mathscr A$, choose a smooth nonconstant real function $h$ of the angular labels carried by the boundary frame. Consider the physically marked area moment $K_h=(8\pi G_N)^{-1}\int_S h\,\mathrm dA$. The test concerns the relative frame on an external corner. A simultaneous change of the bulk fields and the frame is gauge. An angular smearing tied only to arbitrary chart labels would require a relational identification before this test could be applied. [Frame and surface transformations, §3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S3.SS3.p1.1) · [Relative and simultaneous boosts, §3.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S3.SS6.p3.3)

There is a classical reason to compute this commutator. In the displayed double-null symplectic form (A.14), contraction with a variation of the relative normalization at fixed area, angular maps and conformal profiles yields the variation of the corresponding smeared area charge, up to orientation. The mixed terms with the radiative profiles contain no variation of the normalization for that vector. In area coordinates the incoming clock (4.22)–(4.26) uses the conformal profiles, their radial derivatives, and the area density. It has no explicit dependence on the variable conjugate to that density. In the closely related double-null bracket calculation by Reisenberger the area density commutes with the conformal metric and has its nonzero bracket with the relative normalization. His outer-cut conditions differ. The finite cap, its affine normalization, the admissible angular boosts, and the physical quotient must be checked in the present theory. [Manuscript, Appendix A.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#A1.SS2.p2.2) · [Quantum clock](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS3.p4.1) · [Reisenberger, Eqs. (7)–(10)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0712.2541#page=3)

**A conditional obstruction.** Let $P=\mathbf 1_{[0,\ell]}(\widehat{\bar u}_*)$ and suppose $P\Omega\ne0$ for the manuscript's incoming Gaussian. If $K_h$ has a non-atomic spectral measure in $\Omega$ and its spectral projections $E_h(B)$ commute with $P$, the diamond has infinite rank. Indeed
$$
\mu_{P\Omega}(B)=\|E_h(B)P\Omega\|^2
                  =\|PE_h(B)\Omega\|^2
                  \le \mu_\Omega(B).
$$
The left measure is nonzero and contains no atoms. Split it into countably many disjoint sets of positive measure. The normalized vectors proportional to $E_h(B_j)P\Omega$ are orthogonal and satisfy the same clock bound and total-area condition. The argument also works when the projected measure has any nonzero diffuse component. Strong commutation is essential. A formal Poisson bracket on some classical configurations does not establish the required identity for the physical spectral projections. [Manuscript's nonzero projection and quotient, §5.1 and Appendix C.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.E19)

There is a practical one-mode version. In the area parametrization (4.23), take $f_S(y)=xh(y)+f_0(y)$, $w_x(y)=\exp(xh+f_0)/\int_S\exp(xh+f_0)\,\epsilon_0$. Then
$$
\frac{dK_h}{dx}=\frac{\mathscr A}{8\pi G_N}
\left[\int_S h^2w_x\,\epsilon_0-
           \left(\int_S h w_x\,\epsilon_0\right)^2\right]>0.
$$
Consequently a regular Gaussian coordinate $x$ with a continuous conditional density at fixed $f_0$ gives a non-atomic distribution of $K_h$ if this observable acts by multiplication. To settle the question, construct this smeared quantum charge, its action on the renormalized clock form and its spectral measure in a single physical representation. A genuine continuous external shape which commutes with the clock and survives its projection refutes the finite-rank claim. A gauge identification of the shape, a discrete spectrum, or a noncommuting clock action would each require a different account of the local corner variables in §4. The manuscript asserts commutation with **total** area. It does not work through this test for a nonconstant smearing. [Area parametrization](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS3.p4.1) · [Total-area commutation](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS1.p1.1)

A complementary test requires only one-graviton probabilities. Let $e_j=a_j^\dagger\Omega$ be the manuscript's ambient orthonormal one-graviton states and $p_j=\|P e_j\|^2$ the probability that the prepared state passes the fixed clock bound. For any finite $N$, Bessel's inequality gives
$$
\sum_{j=1}^N p_j\le\operatorname{rank}P .
$$
Thus a divergent sum over angular or radial modes at a fixed physical clock bound would rule out finite rank **even if the projected states overlap**. Summability in the one-graviton sector alone leaves the higher occupations and edge sectors to be tested. On a complete ambient orthonormal basis the sum equals the rank. The same spectral projector must be used throughout, or its regulated approximants must be compared under controlled embeddings. [Fixed-area occupation states](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS2.p2.3) · [Clock selection](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS1.p2.1)

## Which sequences of operations can the finite diamond perform?

In finite dimension $d>0$, $\mathrm{Tr}[Q,\Pi]=0$ excludes $[Q,\Pi]=i\hbar I$. The bounded Weyl formulation gives the same obstruction. If two unitary $d\times d$ matrices obey $UV=e^{-i\theta}VU$, their determinants require $e^{-id\theta}=1$. The continuum Weyl relations require arbitrary $\theta$. The manuscript imposes them before projecting. Writing $R=1-P$, its Eq. (C.21) gives
$$
PXY P=(PXP)(PYP)+PXRY P.
$$
A physical measurement of the clock after $Y$ that selects only allowed readings changes the experiment by the second term. For a unitary source $U$, $\|RU\psi\|^2=1-\|PU\psi\|^2$ on normalized allowed states. This loss probability is a direct way to specify a regime where repeated use of finite matrices approximates endpoint-only amplitudes. [Weyl relation, Eq. (4.20)](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.E20) · [Compressed product, Eq. (C.21)](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#A3.E21X)

An exact example shows what is at stake. Within the fixed-area Fock space, choose an allowed unit vector $\psi$ and, if the clock projection $P$ there has finite rank, arbitrarily many mutually orthogonal unit vectors $e_j$ in its complement. Let $U_j$ swap $\psi$ with $e_j$ and fix their orthogonal complement. The established fixed-area von Neumann closure contains these unitaries. All have the same compression $PU_jP=P-|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$, yet
$$
|\langle\psi|U_iU_j|\psi\rangle|^2=\delta_{ij}.
$$
The return experiment distinguishes as many source histories as desired. Compressed one-step matrices lose all information about which history occurred. This is an algebraic consequence for the weakly closed algebra. The paper would have to assign genuinely implementable sources, durations and localization to these ideal swaps before attributing the protocol to an observer inside one diamond. For proposed laboratory sources a concrete alternative is to bound $\|(1-P)U P_{\rm low}\|$ on a specified low-energy subspace $P_{\rm low}\le P$ and propagate that bound over the actual sequence. [Fixed-area matrix units](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#A3.SS3) · [Allowed intermediate states in source words](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS1.p17.1)

## Which boost laws and locality tests survive the projection?

Bulk tetrad Lorentz transformations acting on all fields and the frame are gauge in this construction. A physical relative normal-frame boost is a one-parameter abelian group. It can act exactly on a finite-dimensional space via $U(\eta)=\exp[i\eta\widehat{\mathscr A}/(8\pi G_N\hbar)]$, because the area preserves the clock bound. The familiar obstruction to a nontrivial finite-dimensional continuous unitary representation of the full connected Lorentz group concerns a different claim. Most spacetime Lorentz transformations also change a designated finite diamond or its reference data. To test covariance under such transformations, specify the map between the two diamonds' states and the transformation of their dressed observables. [Gauge-frame distinction](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S3.SS3.p2.3) · [Area phases](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS2.p5.1)

Finite rank makes a sharper prediction for **the relative boost that the paper actually uses**. In the single diamond, a fixed state's matrix element of a conjugated bounded operator, or the doubled collar expectation with the boost acting on the selected right factor, depends on $\eta$ through a finite linear combination of
$$
\exp\!\left[i\eta(\mathscr A_j-\mathscr A_i)/(8\pi G_N\hbar)\right].
$$
These functions have arbitrarily accurate recurrences. The freely falling vacuum expression in Eq. (5.29), $4e^\eta/(1+e^\eta)^2=\mathrm{sech}^2(\eta/2)$, decays at large rapidity. These statements are compatible in the manuscript's finite semiclassical window. The smearing, both boosted probe locations, and the distance to the end of the collar must stay in that window. Compute the allowed area gaps and matrix-element weights and show they reproduce (5.29) within its smearing, curvature and loop errors there. A claim that the smooth decay is exact for all rapidities in this same finite-dimensional system would be false. The paper gives a local semiclassical calculation. [Scale hierarchy and relative-boost correlator](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS2.p6.2) · [Doubled boost expectation](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS2.p10.2)

For locality, §4.1 orders deformations by time and explicitly allows spatially nonlocal dependence. Appendix D.2 invokes the contact support of the **linearized** shear commutator for its specified disjoint smearings. A gravity-specific test would compare matrix elements of the commutator of two relationally dressed, spacelike separated, smoothly smeared probes to the Peierls bracket for the same dressing. Take $\ell_P\ll$ probe width $\ll$ separation $\ll$ curvature radius, keep both frame paths and endpoints fixed in the comparison, and evaluate the leading dressing correction. Then repeat the calculation with the clock projection on every intermediate state. Their difference is exactly $PXRY P-PYRX P$ for the commutator of two bounded insertions $X,Y$. A vanishing free commutator alone supplies no estimate of either contribution. The perturbative flat-space dressing result is a benchmark in its own boundary conditions. [Source support and ordering](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS1.p3.1) · [Linearized commutator](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#A4.SS2.p2.7) · [Donnelly and Giddings](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.01025)

## Does the construction implement quantum error correction or recover continuum gravity?

Quantum error correction is cited in the introduction as motivation from AdS/CFT. To establish it for these diamonds, identify a code of low-energy states, an encoding isometry $V$, a physical boundary region that can be erased, and its observable algebra including corner charges. For a logical algebra $\mathcal M$, exact erasure correction requires $V^\dagger E V\in\mathcal M'$ for all bounded operators $E$ on the erased factor when a factor description is available. For full logical information the right side consists of scalars. A direct reconstruction test is to exhibit, on each of two admissible boundary regions $A,B$, operators obeying $O_A V=O_B V=VO$ for a noncommuting pair of logical observables. State the error in these identities if reconstruction is approximate. If $A$ and $B$ are disjoint with commuting physical algebras, exact recovery of the same noncommuting pair on both is impossible. Overlapping regions can evade that obstruction. With constrained boundary algebras, specify the commutants and shared center explicitly. Global irreducibility or a connected state expressible on two disconnected components establishes no erasure identity on its own. [Motivation](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S1.p3.1) · [Topology construction, §5.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS4) · [Almheiri, Dong and Harlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.7041) · [Harlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.03901)

Two limits answer distinct referee questions. At fixed physical $G_N,\hbar,\epsilon,\ell$, remove the regulators and establish a finite rank for the same clock projection. Then specify a macroscopic sequence, for example $\ell/\ell_P\to\infty$ with probe scales staying inside it, and construct maps for preparations and measurements. Demand convergence of Gram matrices and few-point functions for any fixed collection of low-energy graviton wave packets, along with controlled projection loss and the dressing commutator above. In particular, simultaneous recovery of the vacuum and $m$ independent one-particle packets needs at least $m+1$ dimensions once their Gram matrix is nonsingular. The nested spectral ranges of a single clock embed as vector spaces. They do not by themselves supply consistent localization and dynamics on overlapping spacetime diamonds. These tests say which results would demonstrate a finite microscopic state count and the separate low-energy continuum interpretation. [Clock restriction](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS1.p2.1) · [Semiclassical dynamics, §6](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S6)
