# Which conclusions require a nonempty, finite causal diamond?

**The short answer.** *Classical gravity, the fixed-area Fock representation, and the explicit graviton pair correlator stand independently of §5, whereas discrete area, black-hole state counts, AdS level bounds, and handle redundancy depend directly on a nonempty, finite-rank clock subspace—or on an equivalent finite closed trace.*

The two claims in §5 answer different physical questions. Section 5.1 asks whether at least one macroscopic preparation has positive probability for a finite clock reading. Section 5.2 asks for an upper bound on the **number** of mutually orthogonal preparations that satisfy that reading. A single normalized wave packet establishes existence for its clock range, yet even probability one puts no upper bound on the dimension. Conversely, the dimension bound holds vacuously if the clock projection vanishes—in which case the proposed normalized density matrix does not exist. Meanwhile, the arguments for genuinely interacting continuum evolution in §4 and for the local one-loop equation in §6 carry their own separate conditions. [Clock projection and target limit](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS1.p1.1) · [Dimension argument](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS2.p11.5) · [Semiclassical expansion](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S6)

## Two tests with different outcomes

Let $T$ be the positive clock associated to the limiting form on the closure $M$ of its finite-form domain. Put $P_\ell=\mathbf1_{[0,\ell]}(T)$ on $M$ and extend by zero on $M^\perp$. The spectral theorem gives $P_\ell\to P_M$ strongly as $\ell\to\infty$. Thus $\sup_\ell\|P_\ell\Omega\|^2=\|P_M\Omega\|^2$. A strictly positive right-hand side suffices for **some** finite $\ell$ in the chosen preparation, while the unit limit in (5.19) requires $\Omega\in M$. Neither statement fixes the clock bound for a specified macroscopic radius. The classical Wigner-tail estimate used in (5.16) is explicitly heuristic for the noncommuting operators. If (5.19) fails for this Gaussian, another preparation or a smaller positive overlap can still establish existence. [Cutoff estimate and footnote](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS1.p13.1) · [Domain analysis](05-1.md)

Suppose $P_\ell\ne0$, that area strongly commutes with the clock, and that the focusing bound (5.21) and positive collar weight (5.23) hold. On $P_\ell\mathcal H$, write $s=\widehat{\mathscr A}/(4G_N\hbar)$ and $s_* = \pi\ell^2/[(1-\epsilon)^2 G_N\hbar]$. For every finite-rank subprojection $F\le P_\ell$,

$$
 e^{-s_*}\operatorname{rank} F\ \le\ \operatorname{Tr}(Fe^{-s}F)\ \le\ \operatorname{rank}F.
$$

Taking the supremum over $F$ gives an equivalence between finite positive corner trace and finite dimension. The area filter $f_b$ in (5.25) is strictly positive and continuous on $[0,s_*]$, so the same equivalence holds for that filtered collar at fixed $b,\ell$. A proof of absolute convergence for an open amplitude between normalizable vectors leaves this closed trace undetermined. On $\ell^2(\mathbb N)$ the identity gives absolutely convergent open contractions by Cauchy–Schwarz, and its trace is infinite. An independently constructed finite closed amplitude agreeing with the ordinary corner trace would suffice. [Area bound and corner operator](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS2.p2.1) · [Area filter](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS2.p4.2) · [Open gluing](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS4.p4.1)

## Research problems in priority order

1. **Construct one predictive continuum amplitude.** First recover a closed symplectic form from the complete finite-cap geometry and a differentiable Hamiltonian for the chosen boundary conditions. Check the exterior derivative and Hamiltonian variation on radiative and edge variations, including the explicit tests of (A.14) and (3.50). Then specify radial and angular regulators, the constraints, Gaussian covariance, allowed boundary data, and any renormalization conditions. Construct the interacting cap propagator on a common domain or as a strong limit after identifying the regulated Hilbert spaces. Compute a matrix element between wave packets with a nonzero Einstein interaction and show that refining with a second regulator and moving the cut give the same result. The finite-harmonic pair correlator provides a normalization check. This would test whether the given source algebra and its representation describe the dynamics of four-dimensional GR. [Finite-cap test](03-3.md) · [Hamiltonian flux](03-4.md) · [Source algebra and evolution](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS2.p8.1) · [Cut test](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS4.p4.1)

2. **Find a finite-reading state.** At a chosen macroscopic radius and area interval, construct at least one nonzero vector with a uniformly bounded regulated positive clock form in a compatible continuum limit. For the specific spherical Gaussian, prove positive overlap with $M$, or prove the stronger unit-overlap limit actually claimed. Estimate the limit uniformly as angular and radial regulators are removed. A divergent expectation in the Gaussian leaves open finite readings, whereas vanishing overlap with $M$ rules them out for that preparation. A definition using subtractions for the shear square must re-establish positivity and the focusing lower bound. [Resolvent calculation](05-1.md) · [Composite shear operator](04-3.md)

3. **Decide finite rank by spectral analysis.** On $M$, finite rank of every $P_\ell$ is equivalent to compactness of $(1+T/t_0)^{-1}$ for any fixed reference time $t_0>0$. Indeed, truncating at $\ell$ gives a finite-rank approximation with error at most $(1+\ell/t_0)^{-1}$, and a compact resolvent has only finitely many eigenvectors above any fixed positive threshold, with multiplicity counted. One route is an estimate showing compact embedding of the form domain with its graph norm into $M$, including both polarizations, varying areas and edge modes. **A falsification test** is an orthonormal family $\psi_j$ with clock form bounded by a common $C>0$. Spectral Markov gives $\langle\psi_j,P_{2C}\psi_j\rangle\ge1/2$ and therefore infinite rank by Bessel's inequality. Either result directly addresses the full quantum claim. Check also which initial areas belong to the same representation. In a common sector with all $P_\ell$ finite rank, $\bigcup_{n\ge1}P_{n t_0}M$ can carry at most countably many eigenvalues of a commuting area. Allowing a different pure extension for each initial area changes what a universal spectrum would mean. [Fixed-area extension](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS2.p8.1) · [Open equivalence question](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS1.p17.1) · [Quadratic cost estimate](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#A4.SS3.p5.1)

4. **Construct a closed amplitude independently and compare cuts.** Fix an exact collar, a bounded boost-sensitive insertion and a clock range. Define its uncut amplitude including normalization, verify positivity where applicable, and prove equality to the directed net of all finite cut projections with the insertion held fixed. One route consistent with D.3 is to derive a regulator-independent positive lower bound on the two-copy return ratio for the exact state. If $0\le W\le I$, then $\operatorname{Tr}[(FWF)^2]/[\operatorname{Tr}(FWF)]^2\le 1/\operatorname{Tr}(FWF)$ for each finite cutoff with positive denominator. A uniform positive return ratio thus bounds the trace from above. The one-loop integral for regular saddles leaves the exact lower bound open. Test different foliations of the same sourced geometry and disjoint component pairings. These tests also determine when E.3's closed amplitude axioms apply. [Cyclic return criterion](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#A4.SS3.p7.1) · [Uncut correlator](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5.SS2.p11.4) · [Allowed pairings](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#A5.I1.i1)

5. **Match an observable whose answer is already constrained by gravity.** Use a weak-field region and external packets with specified polarizations to derive an interacting four-graviton amplitude at tree level. With the large-region, asymptotically flat and infrared prescriptions fixed, compare its loop expansion to the known renormalized two-loop scale dependence. This can distinguish an interacting gravitational prediction from a convergent contraction between vectors. In parallel, an independent Schwarzschild check would compute the normalized replica determinant for the actual clock filter, including boundary, ghost, zero-mode and area-measure terms, and compare the fixed-mass ensemble with angular momenta summed. Neither comparison assumes that the microscopic state count has already been proved.
