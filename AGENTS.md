# Contributions to the null-cone gravity study

## Editorial sequence

Astra leads ongoing calculations, technical review and writing. Use independent Astra checks for consequential reasoning, calculations and experimental claims. Preserve attribution for the Fable reviews and Neon public edits already completed for this edition.

Give the reviewer the exact paper version, the proposed change and its evidence. Ask for counterexamples, missing hypotheses and alternative readings. Resolve factual disagreements and record consequential open objections with their scope. Edit the reviewed draft for accessibility, then check all equations, quotations, references, links and conclusions against it.

## Standards for claims and evidence

Distinguish three kinds of findings plainly: **proof gaps** (an unproved step that leaves the underlying conjecture open), **defects in the construction as written** (internal mismatches requiring a repair to the formulation), and **physical refutations** (evidence against the physical conjecture itself). Keep a model calculation distinct from a result about the full gravitational theory.

Put specified preparations, measurements and testable calculations ahead of historical debate where suitable. For every executable check, document the command and expected output, and verify the suite with `make check`. For a proposed theorem, state the hypotheses and what evidence would settle it. Attribute the original work accurately and link to its versioned arXiv record (`arXiv:2611.34924v1`).

## Handbook and publication checks

When `docs/handbook.tex` changes, rebuild `output/pdf/handbook.pdf` with `make handbook`, check the resulting page count and inspect the typeset equations and page breaks before publication. Verify that repository links resolve to public tracked files and that citations lead to the intended external sources, including the correct arXiv version and equation.
